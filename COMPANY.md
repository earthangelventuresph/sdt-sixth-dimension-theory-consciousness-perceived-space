## Mission
To convert extraordinary-sounding claims about consciousness and perception into testable, falsifiable, publicly examinable research — where evidence, not assertion, decides.

## Vision
A research culture where any claim can be stated clearly, tested honestly, corrected openly, and built upon by others.

## What We Do
- Develop theoretical models with operational definitions and numerical falsification thresholds.
- Publish full protocols before data collection.
- Publish negative / null results with the same care as positive results.
- Invite correction, replication, and independent audit.
- Share methods, materials, and (future) de-identified data under CC BY 4.0.

## What We Do NOT Do
- Do NOT claim data, results, approvals, funding, or partnerships that do not exist.
- Do NOT offer medical, therapeutic, spiritual, or supernatural services.
- Do NOT sell access to a "dimension," travel, powers, or experiences.
- Do NOT present predictions as data, or hypotheses as established facts.
- Do NOT use private / identifiable participant data for publicity.
