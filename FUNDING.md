## What Funding Does NOT Cover / Buy
- Does NOT buy a positive result, a supported verdict, or a changed threshold.
- Does NOT buy authorship, endorsement, or partnership status.
- Does NOT buy access to a dimension, experience, travel, or power.
- Does NOT buy private participant data or identifiable EEG/video.
- Does NOT buy silence about null / negative results.
- Does NOT buy a change to frozen v1.0 thresholds mid-study.
