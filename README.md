## Abstract

**Background.** Human perception is a constructed representation that combines sensory input, memory, attention and interpretation. Accounts of a sixth dimension experienced as a mind-created vision exist in spiritual literature but lack operational definitions. A distinctive statement in the original proposal is that visible clouds indicate that the observed space is not the sixth dimension.

**Objective.** To convert the proposal into a testable theory by deciding what the sixth dimension is, defining the state independently of clouds, formalising the cloud statement as a rule that can fail, and specifying experiments with numerical falsification thresholds.

**Methods.** The theory defines perceived space by six coordinates. The sixth coordinate, G, is the share of a percept that is internally generated. G is measured as a composite of neural decoupling from a frequency-tagged probe, behavioural decoupling from external probes, and reported internal generation. Source-attribution accuracy Q separates the proposed state from hallucination-like content. The state criterion is G ≥ 0.70 and Q ≥ 0.80. Cloud visibility V is measured by calibrated camera. A saturating anchoring model links cloud fraction to a ceiling on G. Four experiments (N = 42, 60, 40 and 40 independent adult participants) test seven hypotheses with exact binomial bounds, equivalence tests and mixed-effects models.

**Results.** No empirical data have been collected. Analytical results of the model are reported separately from data: the critical anchoring strength is 0.773 normalised units, the critical cloud fraction is 0.00251 (below the visibility limit of 0.003), the zero-event sample size for the rule is 59 effective episodes, and the controlled-sky experiment (Experiment 2) is designed to support the rule with probability 0.76 if the true violation rate is 0.02 and with probability below 0.0001 if it is 0.10.

**Interpretation.** If the hypotheses are supported, visible clouds are external anchors that prevent a measurable perceptual state, and the sixth dimension is a perceptual coordinate with no physical correlate. If any pre-specified decision rule fails, the corresponding part of the theory is rejected as stated in the verdict matrix.

**Conclusion.** The Sixth-Dimension Theory is a falsifiable perceptual theory. It is a hypothesis and protocol, not an established dimensional model.

**Keywords:** sixth dimension; consciousness; perception; perceived space; internal generation; source monitoring; sensory anchoring; cloud visibility; falsification.

## Research Significance

### Scientific Problem
Claims that a person can experience a "sixth dimension" as a mind-created vision circulate widely but cannot be evaluated because the term is not defined by any measurement. The scientific problem is to determine whether perceived space contains a measurable internally generated component that behaves as an independent coordinate, whether a defined state of high internal generation can be identified and separated from imagery, dreaming and hallucination, and whether a common environmental feature — visible cloud — systematically limits that state.

### Knowledge Gap
Three gaps are addressed. First, no operational criterion distinguishes a reported sixth-dimension experience from mental imagery, dreams or hallucination-like experience. Second, no test exists of whether a specific, ordinary environmental feature predicts the absence of such a state. Third, proposals that use the phrase "sixth dimension" do not state whether they concern physical space or experience, so they cannot be compared with evidence.

### Original Contribution
The paper contributes a six-coordinate descriptor of perceived space, a numerical criterion for the sixth-dimension state, the Cloud-Visibility Rule stated as a sufficient exclusion that can fail, an anchoring mechanism with explicit parameters, and four experiments with numerical falsification thresholds. Originality is claimed as a claim to be verified. The literature search performed so far is recorded in the manuscript and does not replace a systematic review.

### Potential Importance
If supported, the theory would show that visible external structure measurably limits internally generated experience and would provide a method for converting dimension claims about consciousness into testable statements. If rejected, the pre-specified decision rules would show exactly which element failed. Both outcomes add knowledge. No practical or therapeutic claim is made in this version.

## Formal Model

### Postulate 1 — Constructed and Incomplete Perception
Ordinary perception gives limited access to environmental information. Each percept is a constructed combination of sensory input, memory, attention and other cognitive variables, mixed from a sensory-driven and an internally generated component.

- Eq. 2: `P(t) = f[S(t), M(t), A(t), C(t)]`
- Eq. 3: `P(t) = [1 − G(t)]·PS(t) + G(t)·PI(t)`, with `0 ≤ G ≤ 1`
- Status: first clause established; decomposition is a modelling hypothesis.

### Postulate 2 — Six-Coordinate Perceived Space
A percept of space is described by six coordinates. The sixth is the internal-generation share G. The coordinates are statistically separable.

- Eq. 1: `q = (x1, x2, x3, x4, x5, x6)`, with `x6 = G`
- x1–x3 = perceived width, height, depth (metres, magnitude estimation)
- x4 = perceived temporal continuity (reproduced / actual duration)
- x5 = contextual significance (index, 0–1)
- x6 = G (index, 0–1)
- "Dimension" here means an independent degree of freedom of the percept descriptor — not a position in physical space.

### Postulate 3 — The Sixth-Dimension State
An observer is in the sixth-dimension state when the percept is dominated by internally generated content (G high) **and** the observer correctly attributes that content to the mind (Q high). The criterion uses no cloud measure.

- Eq. 4: `R = 1 − min(1, Astate / Abase)` — neural decoupling, 6 Hz SSVEP
- Eq. 5: `B = 1 − min(1, d′state / d′base)` — behavioural decoupling
- Eq. 6: `U = (PSQ − 6) / 36` — reported internal generation, PSQ-6 total 6–42
- Eq. 7: `G = wR·R + wU·U + wB·B`, with `wR + wU + wB = 1`
- Eq. 8: `Q = ncorrect / 16` — source-attribution accuracy
- Eq. 9: `D = 1 if G ≥ G* and Q ≥ Q*; otherwise D = 0`
- `G* = 0.70`, `Q* = 0.80` (13 of 16 correct; chance probability 0.0106)
- Evaluated once per 90-second state window.
- Calibration rule: after Experiment 1, G* becomes the larger of 0.70 and the 95th percentile of G across passive-viewing and imagery trials. The final G* is frozen and registered before Experiment 2.

### Postulate 4 — Cloud-Visibility Rule
When clouds are visible to the observer, the observed space is not the sixth dimension. Visible cloud is sufficient to exclude the state. Absence of visible cloud is necessary for the state but is never sufficient and never counts as evidence for it.

- Eq. 10: `V = 1 if c ≥ cV and Cw ≥ Cmin; otherwise V = 0`
- Eq. 19: `Cw = |Lc − Ls| / Ls` — Weber contrast
- Eq. 11: `V = 1 → D = 0` (equivalently `D = 1 → V = 0`)
- `cV = 0.003`, `Cmin = 0.02`
- c = cloud area / unoccluded sky area within 60° of the forward axis.
- Applies to episodes in which V is constant from the start of sky settle to the end of the state window (210 s).
- Status: Hypothesis. The original distinctive statement, preserved as a defining hypothesis. It is not an established scientific criterion.

### Postulate 5 — Anchoring Mechanism
Visible external structure acts as an anchor to the sensory world. Anchoring lowers the achievable share of internal generation. Cloud supplies anchoring that saturates at small cloud fractions, so every visible cloud raises anchoring above the critical value.

- Eq. 12: `Λ(c) = Λ0 + Λc·[1 − exp(−c / c0)]`
- Eq. 13: `G ≤ Gmax / [1 + (Λ / Λ50)^h]` — holds in expectation; individual trials carry measurement noise, hence tolerance ε
- Eq. 14: `Λ* = Λ50·[(Gmax / G*) − 1]^(1/h) = 0.773`
- Eq. 15: `ccrit = −c0·ln[1 − (Λ* − Λ0) / Λc] = 0.00251`, required: `ccrit ≤ cV`
- Status: Hypothesis with stipulated parameters. If H4 fails, the rule may remain as a description but the mechanism is rejected.

### Postulate 6 — Perceptual Scope
The sixth dimension is a perceptual-representational coordinate. The theory does not claim that a physical dimension exists. Physical environmental variables are independent of the state.

- Eq. 23: `dj = (mj,1 − mj,0) / sj`, with `|dj| < 0.30` for every measured variable j
- Status: Hypothesis (null prediction). A physical claim would need a separate mathematical-physical model and is not made here.

## Extended Mechanisms

### Generalised Anchoring (Eq. 24)
Cloud is one external visual structure. For any set of structures i: `Λ = Λ0 + Σi Λi·[1 − exp(−ci / c0,i)]`. The theory predicts that a phase-scrambled texture with the same amplitude spectrum, mean luminance and RMS contrast as cloud anchors equally (H6). The Cloud-Visibility Rule is not weakened if this holds, because cloud remains a sufficient exclusion.

### Visible vs. Physically Present Cloud
Visibility is defined by Eq. 10 and Eq. 19. Cloud occluded by a roof or canopy is outside the unoccluded sky and does not enter c. Cloud at twilight, behind haze, or with contrast below 0.02 has V = 0 even though it is physically present, and the rule makes no statement about it. Observers use corrected vision; trials with glare, sun in field, or gaze away from the sky region are flagged and handled by exclusion rules.

### Conditions Without Visible Cloud
At night, with eyes closed, or indoors, V = 0. The rule is silent there. Such conditions neither support nor challenge the rule and are not used to test it. Because D = 1 requires V = 0 but V = 0 does not imply D = 1, an absence of cloud is never counted as evidence for the state.

### Individual Differences
Vividness of imagery differs between persons, including people with aphantasia. VVIQ scores are recorded and entered as covariates. Participants are not excluded for low vividness. The theory does not require every person to reach D = 1. It requires that the rate in clear conditions exceed 0.20 (H2) and that no verified episode occur under visible cloud beyond tolerance (H3).

### Episode Definition and Temporal Stability
An episode is one 300-second trial. The rule is evaluated for episodes in which V is unchanged from the start of sky settle to the end of the state window. In natural sky, an episode counts for H3 only if V is unchanged for at least 95% of video frames over that interval. Transitions of V within a trial are recorded and reported but are not used in any decision rule.

## Experiments and Falsification Thresholds

### Shared Measurement Specification
- **Dome:** Hemispherical projection dome, 2.4 m diameter, 160° field, luminance-calibrated (mean scene luminance 150 cd/m²). Participant reclined, head fixed, eyes open. Cloud scenes static, luminance-matched to clear scenes within 5%, Weber contrast 0.10 unless stated.
- **Frequency-tagged probe:** 6 Hz flicker, 4° disc, Michelson contrast 10%, three 4 s bursts per state window + 3 min baseline on neutral grey. 32-channel EEG at 1000 Hz. Astate / Abase = mean 6 Hz amplitude over Oz, O1, O2.
- **External probes:** Faint luminance increment, 2° disc, 100 ms, intensity set per participant by staircase to 75% detection at baseline. Per state window: 8 real probes + 8 imagined-cue intervals (tone whose stereo position tells participant where to imagine a marker). Button press on real probe. d′ uses log-linear corrected hit / false-alarm rates. Baseline d′ from 64 intervals on neutral grey.
- **Source test (Q):** After window, 16 events in random order. Participant states "displayed" or "imagined". `Q = ncorrect / 16`.
- **PSQ-6 (U):** Items rated 1–7: (1) The space I experienced was created by my own mind. (2) The space felt larger than my physical surroundings. (3) I stayed aware of the display or sky in front of me (reverse-scored). (4) What I experienced did not come from anything in front of me. (5) I could attend to parts of the space without moving my eyes. (6) I was clear that the experience was generated by me. If Cronbach alpha < 0.70 in Experiment 1, U is dropped and weights become wR = 4/7, wB = 3/7.
- **MVIS-1 guided script (90 s):** "Please rest your gaze on the centre of the scene and keep your eyes open. Breathe slowly and let your body be still. Allow your attention to widen. Now let a space begin to form in front of you that is made by your own mind, separate from what is physically in the room. Let it grow wider and deeper at its own pace. You do not need to describe it or make it clear. If it fades, allow it to return."
- **Exclusions / safety:** See protocols. Adults 18–65 only. No payment tied to reaching a state. On-call clinical psychologist. Sessions end on distress.

## Discussion / Conclusion

The theory turns a descriptive claim about a sixth dimension into a perceptual hypothesis with a quantitative state criterion. The Cloud-Visibility Rule has a coherent rationale: visible cloud is a strong external anchor, and an observer held to the senses cannot be dominated by internally generated content. The rule cannot be satisfied by definition, because the state is measured without reference to cloud and a single cell of the logic grid is prohibited.

**Limits:** Parameter values are stipulated and tested, not derived. Composite G rests on a neural probe, behavioural probe and questionnaire whose validity is tested by Experiment 1. Dome reproduces cloud as visual scene but not weather, so Experiment 4 is included. Tests use frequency-tagged flicker, which restricts participants through safety screening. Rule is stated for steady visibility and does not address transitions.

**What the theory does NOT claim:** No physical sixth dimension. No spiritual / supernatural interpretation. No clinical or therapeutic benefit. No result. Not every person can reach the state.

**Conclusion:** The Sixth-Dimension Theory defines the sixth dimension as the internally generated share of perceived space, defines the state independently of clouds, preserves the original cloud-visibility statement as a rule that can fail, supplies an anchoring mechanism, and states the experiments, numerical thresholds and verdicts that would confirm or reject each part. It is a hypothesis and protocol. No data exist, and the theory remains unestablished until the registered experiments are completed and independently replicated.
