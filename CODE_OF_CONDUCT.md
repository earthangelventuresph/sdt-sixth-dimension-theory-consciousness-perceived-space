# Code of Conduct — Earth Angel Ventures Research

> No empirical data have been collected for EAV-SDT-001. No results are claimed. Ethics not yet approved.
> Earth Angel Ventures Research Standard: "The standard is not how extraordinary a claim sounds, but how reliably the evidence supports it and how well others can examine, challenge, reproduce, and build upon the work."
> Verse: 1 Corinthians 10:31 | Philippians 2:13

## Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this research community welcoming, respectful, and honest for everyone — regardless of background, belief, or level of expertise. Criticism of ideas is welcome. Harassment is not.

## Standards — Expected
- Be honest: distinguish hypothesis, evidence, and opinion.
- Be precise: cite file, section, and line when correcting.
- Be respectful: no personal attacks, threats, or demeaning language.
- Be open: welcome replication, correction, and null results.
- Protect participants: never share identifiable participant information.
- Do not misrepresent: do not present predictions as data or hypotheses as proven.

## Standards — Unacceptable
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualised language, or personal attacks.
- Impersonation of Earth Angel Ventures, Angelica Pangan, or any researcher.
- Selling access, claiming partnership, or claiming endorsement that does not exist.
- Posting identifiable participant data, private contact details, or doxxing.
- Spamming, trolling, or bad-faith disruption.

## Scope
This Code applies to GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, and any space representing this repository, as well as related email / social contact about this research.

## Enforcement
- **Report:** earthangelventuresph@gmail.com — Subject: "Code of Conduct Report — EAV-SDT-001" + links + dates.
- **Response:** Maintainer reviews, may warn, hide / remove content, restrict participation, or ban.
- **Appeal:** Reply to the decision email within 14 days with new information.
- **No retaliation** for good-faith reports.
