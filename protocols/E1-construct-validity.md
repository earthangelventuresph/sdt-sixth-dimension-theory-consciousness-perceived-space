## Claim in Plain Language
Perceived space has a measurable sixth coordinate, G, that is separable from the other five coordinates, and a sixth-dimension state (high G + high Q) exists, can be induced, and can be distinguished from ordinary viewing, mental imagery, and hallucination-like content.

## Formal Prediction
- **H1:** r(G, xj) for j = 1–5 lies inside (−0.80, 0.80) with 95% confidence.
- **H2:** State occurs in > 20% of induction trials (lower 95% bound) AND mean G in induction exceeds mean G in imagery by > 0.10.
- Predicted G ranges: imagery 0.20–0.60; induction higher; Ganzfeld positive control high G.

## Falsification Threshold — Decided in Advance
- H1 rejected if any r(G, xj) 95% CI lies outside (−0.80, 0.80).
- H2 rejected if lower 95% bound of induction rate ≤ 0.20 OR induction-minus-imagery G difference ≤ 0.10.
- If H1 or H2 rejected: Postulates 2 and 3 withdrawn; remaining tests moot (verdict matrix).

## Design
- Within-participant, counterbalanced conditions: passive viewing, mental imagery, guided induction (MVIS-1), Ganzfeld positive control.
- Each participant completes multiple 300 s trials: 30 s sky settle → 90 s guided induction → 90 s state window → 60 s report → 30 s rest.
- Total: 1,008 trials across 42 participants (24 trials per participant, as specified in manuscript tables).
- No participant takes part in any other experiment.

## Apparatus / Materials
- Hemispherical projection dome, 2.4 m diameter, 160° field, mean scene luminance 150 cd/m², reclined chair, head fixed, eyes open.
- 6 Hz frequency-tagged probe: 4° disc, Michelson contrast 10%, three 4 s bursts per state window + 3 min baseline on neutral grey.
- 32-channel EEG at 1000 Hz; SSVEP amplitude over Oz, O1, O2.
- External probes: 2° disc, 100 ms, staircase to 75% detection at baseline. Per window: 8 real + 8 imagined-cue intervals.
- Response box, tablet, eye tracker, PSQ-6, VVIQ, MVIS-1 script.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Screen, consent (after ethics approval + registration), VVIQ, baseline EEG / d′ (64 intervals, neutral grey).
2. Calibrate external probe intensity per participant (staircase to 75%).
3. For each trial: sky settle (30 s) → condition instruction / MVIS-1 (90 s) → state window (90 s) with 3 probe bursts + 16 events → report: PSQ-6 + source test (16 items, random order) → rest (30 s).
4. Repeat per counterbalanced order. Breaks as needed. Session ends on distress.
5. Automatic pipeline computes R, U, B, G, Q, D. No human scoring of D.

## Sample Size + Power
- **N = 42 independent adults (18–65).**
- Paired design. Eq. 20: `N = [(z1−α/2 + z1−β) / dz]^2`, t-correction. Planning dz = 0.50, α = 0.05, β = 0.20 (z = 1.960, 0.842).
- G* calibration: after E1, G* = max(0.70, 95th percentile of G across passive-viewing + imagery trials). Frozen + registered before E2. Eq. 14–15 recomputed. H4(b) evaluated against frozen G*.

## Blinding
- D scored automatically, blind to condition labels where applicable. Analysis pipeline pre-registered. Condition log sealed until analysis.
- PSQ-6: if Cronbach alpha < 0.70, U dropped; weights become wR = 4/7, wB = 3/7.

## Frozen Analysis Plan
- Compute R, B, U, G, Q per window per Eq. 4–9.
- H1: correlations r(G, xj) with 95% CIs.
- H2: induction rate with 95% lower bound; paired G induction vs imagery.
- Holm correction for multiple tests where specified in manuscript.
- No exploratory re-analysis changes a decision rule.

## Confounds
- Attention modulation of SSVEP; order effects (counterbalancing); imagery vividness (VVIQ covariate); demand characteristics (objective R/B weighted above self-report U); fatigue (breaks, counterbalancing).
- Alternative explanations excluded: low vividness is not an exclusion; theory does not require every person to reach D = 1.
