## Claim in Plain Language
The Cloud-Visibility Rule holds outside the dome, under a real sky. Visible cloud in nature also excludes the sixth-dimension state, and the state still shows no physical environmental correlate.

## Formal Prediction
- Same as E2: strong form k = 0; operational form pU ≤ 0.05.
- Predicted P(D = 1 | V = 0) ~0.40; P(D = 1 | V = 1) ~0.
- H7: |dj| < 0.30 for all five environmental sensors.

## Falsification Threshold — Decided in Advance
- Same as E2. If fewer than 59 effective V = 1 episodes achieved, Eq. 16 cannot reach 0.05 and operational form is rejected for E4.
- H7 rejected if any 90% CI of dj lies outside (−0.30, +0.30).

## Design
- Field study, open site in the Philippines with ≥ 120° unobstructed sky, no overhead structure.
- Sessions scheduled across 12 weeks without regard to weather. Trials classified afterwards by measured V.
- Stopping rule: stop when ≥ 300 usable V = 1 trials AND ≥ 100 usable V = 0 trials exist, OR at week 12, whichever comes first.
- Up to 3 sessions per participant, 8 trials per session (300 s each). Up to 960 trials, N = 40.

## Apparatus / Materials
- Reclined chair with head support, portable EEG + probe unit on visor, response box, tablet, eye tracker, calibrated head-position camera, all-sky camera, photometer, environmental sensors, controller.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Screen, consent (after approval + registration), baseline, calibration at site.
2. Each session: 8 trials, all with MVIS-1. Sky settle → induction → state window → report → rest.
3. All-sky camera records continuously. Trials where V changes during > 5% of frames are NOT used for H3 (recorded and reported).
4. Sun in field flagged; analysed as sensitivity case.
5. Audit every V = 1 + D = 1 episode as in E2.

## Sample Size + Power
- **N = 40 new independent adults (18–65).** Up to 960 trials.
- Target: ≥ 300 usable V = 1 + ≥ 100 usable V = 0 trials. If target not reached by week 12, operational form rejected for E4 per pre-set rule.

## Blinding
- D automatic, blind to V. V automatic from camera, blind to D. Camera V agreement with two trained human observers: kappa ≥ 0.80 required for V validity check.

## Frozen Analysis Plan
- As E2: k, neff, pU. Solar elevation, wind, time of day as covariates. Sun-in-field sensitivity analysis.
- H2(a): P(D = 1 | V = 0). H7: dj per sensor.

## Confounds
- Weather covariates (temperature, wind, noise); sun glare (flag + sensitivity); human vs camera cloud judgement (kappa); horizon landmarks (counted in Λ0); heat / rain / lightning (safety stops, not data exclusions unless pre-specified).

## Data Availability
- No data exist. Future field logs, camera data, sensor data deposited with registration, subject to privacy limits.
